## Projects

### [TalentVP](https://talentvp.com)
Career platform for international professionals looking for work in Germany, Austria and Switzerland. I am the founder and the only engineer. (2025 to today)

- CV analysis, CV tailoring for a specific job ad, cover letters, mock interviews with STAR feedback and job tracking. Available in English, German and Turkish.
- All AI writes to a user's CV go through a single entry point. An output check blocks rewrites that damage the text, and every suspected invented claim is traced back to the original CV.
- Test set of sample personas. A prompt change only goes live after it is measured against them.
- Moved every route from GPT-4 to GPT-4o-mini and cut model costs by 95 to 98 percent without losing quality in the tests. Trimming the prompt input saved another 96 percent of tokens per call. Added a hash cache and repair of broken output instead of a full new generation.
- TalentVP Copilot: a Chrome extension in the Chrome Web Store. It shows how well your CV fits a job ad directly on LinkedIn, StepStone and company career pages.
- Stack: Next.js, TypeScript, Prisma, PostgreSQL, NextAuth, Stripe, OpenAI and Anthropic APIs, Playwright, Docker on a server in Munich.
